# Cannabis-use levels and neurocognition among people living with HIV

**Dissertation study 2 · Manuscript under review**

*Current manuscript title:* Effects of cannabis on neurocognition: Moderating effects of disease severity among people living with HIV.

## Research context

Whether cannabis use relates to neurocognition in people living with HIV depends partly on what is meant by *use*. This follow-up study examines differences in global and domain-specific neurocognition across groups defined by **amount of cannabis used over the past year**, while considering HIV disease severity and demographic characteristics. It builds on the published [joint-effects study](https://doi.org/10.1037/neu0001003), which showed why broad cannabis-use categories and overall average performance can miss more specific distinctions.

In these analyses, associations are not uniform across cannabis-use groups or cognitive outcomes. The lower-use group should not be interpreted as demonstrating a cognitive benefit over nonuse; nor was HIV disease severity found to significantly modify the associations in the analyses reported to date. These are observational findings, not causal effects.

The manuscript title and public project summary will be updated if a revised title is approved. This repository name is retained for continuity.

## Repository contents and limitations

This repository holds **working R analysis files**, including scripts for setup, data preparation, construction of an HIV disease-severity factor, descriptive statistics, statistical models, and tables or figures. These files contain iterative and exploratory analysis code; their current contents may not correspond exactly to the manuscript's final statistical specification. They have **not** been validated as a complete, independently executable replication workflow.

The analyses rely on restricted participant-level data that are **not provided publicly**. File paths referring to source datasets are not download links or evidence that study data are shared here.
